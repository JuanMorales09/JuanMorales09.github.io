02 / Ways to work together

The right website.
The right scope.

Focused informational websites that introduce your business and help customers reach you.

Start with the essentials

One-page website

A focused home for your business online.

  • Introduction and service sections
  • About your business
  • Contact and location links
  • Responsive mobile layout
Give your business more room

Business website

A fuller introduction to what you offer.

  • Up to five informational pages
  • Dedicated service information
  • Project or photo gallery
  • Page titles and descriptions
Make a fresh impression

Website refresh

A new direction for an outdated website.

  • Review of your current content
  • Updated layout and navigation
  • Improved mobile presentation
  • Clearer contact links

Every project starts with a written quote defining pages, content, revisions, and timeline. Domain, hosting, and ongoing updates are quoted separately. These services cover informational websites; online payments, accounts, and custom back-end systems are outside this scope.
